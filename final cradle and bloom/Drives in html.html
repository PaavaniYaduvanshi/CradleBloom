On The Ground

Urgent Action Needed Today

Our active drives are high-priority initiatives providing immediate relief, essential supplies, and direct aid to vulnerable women and children.

Drives for children

Books for Every Backpack

Many kids share one worn textbook between three or four classmates. This drive puts a full set of textbooks and a few storybooks into every backpack at three primary schools.

1 Lakh+ donations raised so far.

School meals program

A hot lunch is the difference between a child who can focus in class and one who's counting minutes until they get home to eat. This covers daily meals for a full term.

2 Lakh+ donations raised so far.

Drives for women

Period drive

Many women miss work or school because they can't afford pads or tampons. This drive keeps a full year of supplies stocked at local shelters.

1 Lakh+ donations raised so far.

Career closet drive

One good outfit can be the difference between getting a job and missing the opportunity.

2 Lakh+ donations raised so far.

Financial Literacy & Microfinance Drives

Teaching budgeting, savings, and connecting women to microloans or SHGs (Self-Help Groups).

Entrepreneurship Support Drives

Mentorship, funding access, and market linkage for women-run small businesses.
