A mother given a way forward

August 28, 2026

When Meera first came to our community centre, she carried more than the small bag in her hands. She was carrying the responsibility of raising her young daughter while struggling to find stable work.

After losing her source of income, even basic necessities such as school supplies and groceries had become difficult to manage.

“I did not want someone to change my life for me. I only needed a chance to change it myself.”

Through the Craddle & Bloom women's support programme, Meera joined a small vocational training course where she learned tailoring and basic business skills.

During her training, our education programme also helped provide books and school materials for her daughter. This allowed Meera to focus on building a more stable future for both of them.

A few months later, Meera began taking small tailoring orders from families in her neighbourhood. What started with a few pieces of clothing slowly became a regular source of income.

What your support made possible

Donations helped provide training materials, educational supplies and temporary assistance while Meera worked towards financial independence.

Today, Meera continues to grow her work while her daughter attends school with everything she needs. Her story reminds us that sometimes lasting change begins with something very simple — an opportunity.
