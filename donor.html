Why I Give Monthly — A Donor's Story

August 28, 2026

Every month, without fail, Rahul makes time to donate blood. He doesn't consider himself a hero. For him, it is simply something he can do to help someone who may need it. Rahul first began donating blood after seeing how difficult it was for families to find the right blood type during emergencies. What started as a single donation became a monthly commitment. Through his regular donations, Rahul has helped Craddle & Bloom support women and children who require blood during medical emergencies and treatments.

One of those people was Meena, a young mother whose child needed an urgent blood transfusion. While Meena's family was already struggling with medical expenses, finding a compatible blood donor became another source of fear. A blood unit from one of our registered donors helped provide the support the family desperately needed. Rahul never met Meena. He may never know her name. But every month, when he rolls up his sleeve and donates again, he is giving someone else a little more time, a little more hope, and a chance to recover.

His monthly habit became someone else's lifeline.

Donate blood. Give hope. Save a life.
