A New Beginning for Meera

August 28, 2026

Ten-year-old Riya had always wanted to go to school, but her family struggled to afford even basic necessities. Her parents worked irregular jobs, and buying school supplies was often impossible.

When Craddle & Bloom met Riya, she was attending school with old notebooks and borrowed stationery. Sometimes, she would miss classes because she didn't have the required materials.

Our team provided her with a school uniform, books, stationery and a school bag. For Riya, the new school bag wasn't simply a gift. It meant she could walk into her classroom without worrying about being different from the other children. Today, Riya rarely misses school. She loves science and dreams of becoming a doctor.

One child. One opportunity. A completely different future.
